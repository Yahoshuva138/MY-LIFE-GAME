CHAPTER 01
◆

THE ORIGIN

The story begins with two places.

Hassanabad — the native village, where the roots of this story belong.

Sattenapalli — the nearby town where Yahoshuva was born, because there was no hospital in Hassanabad.

After his birth, his family shifted to Sattenapalli.

Two places. One beginning.

One would represent his roots. The other would become the setting for the next chapter of his early life.

The world was still simple.

No legendary quests. No powerful weapons. No massive open worlds.

Just childhood, family, curiosity… and little moments that would eventually become memories.
