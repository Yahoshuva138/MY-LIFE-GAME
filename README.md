# YAHOSHUVA: ORIGIN ZERO
> **"Every legend has a beginning."**  
> *"He didn't start with extraordinary abilities. He developed them through the worlds he explored."*

A cinematic 3D Open-World Life RPG & anime-inspired adventure where real-life experience unlocks character progression.

---

## 🎮 The Experience

1. **Title Screen (`index.html`)**
   - Cinematic darkness to dawn transition
   - Atmospheric procedural audio (synthesized via Web Audio API)
   - Golden particle engine & Hassanabad mountain silhouette
   - Impactful title strike & pulsing prompt

2. **Storyline 01: The First Spark (`storyline01.html`)**
   - 16-scene visual novel / cinematic prologue
   - Real-world setting: Hassanabad & Sattenapalli (13 March 2007)
   - Typewriter narration & Ken Burns background camera pan
   - Procedural heartbeat, birth chimes, low-frequency rumbles
   - RPG Origin System Awakening & Player Card (Level 01)

3. **Official Codex & Master Story Bible (`storybible.html` & `STORY_BIBLE_PART_01.md`)**
   - Central Philosophy & Core Themes
   - Character Evolution Tree (Ordinary Student $\rightarrow$ Gamer $\rightarrow$ Anime Enthusiast $\rightarrow$ Practical Learner)
   - Complete 11-Level Chronology & Arcs
   - The Dual Dimensions (Gaming & Anime)
   - Signature Real Quotes vs. Proposed Dialogue Vault
   - 4-Pillar Game Engine Architecture (Cutscene, Playable Memory, Mission, Unlock)
