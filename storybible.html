Identity & World

  • Game Title: YAHOSHUVA: ORIGIN ZERO
  • Tagline: Every legend has a beginning.
  • Genre: Cinematic Open-World RPG / Anime-Inspired Adventure / Life Sim
  • Origins: Hassanabad & Sattenapalli, Andhra Pradesh
  • Protagonist: Yahoshuva (Born 13 March 2007)

The Realistic Hero Arc

Most RPGs assign the protagonist a prophecy, ancient bloodline, or supernatural birth. In ORIGIN ZERO, the power curve mirrors real life: curiosity, routine, lockdown boredom channeled into reflex gaming, linguistic endurance through subtitled anime, practical data responsibility in a rural school, and hands-on engineering lab work.

PILLAR 03

STORY LEVELS (01 TO 11)

PROLOGUE

The Origin (13 March 2007)

Hassanabad (native roots) has no hospital. Birth occurs in Sattenapalli; family moves there. Two places, one destiny.

LVL 01

The Ordinary Student

Primary education. A normal student moving through exams, homework, and simple memories.

LVL 02

The Corona Dimension

COVID-19 pandemic shuts the world down. Isolation creates the fertile ground for two great explorations: Gaming and Anime.

LVL 03

First Personal Device

Graduating from family phones to a 2GB RAM / 32GB ROM Android tablet. Personal digital awakening.

LVL 04

The Starter Game Collection

Subway Surfers, Temple Run, Jetpack Joyride, Plants vs. Zombies 1 & 2. Reflexes, defense, timing.

LVL 05

The GOAT Collection: LIMBO & Leo's Fortune

Artistic revelation. Darkness, gravity puzzles, atmosphere. "Not every challenge can be solved by running."

LVL 06

Free Fire & The Hardware Boss

Competitive battle royale. The ultimate enemy isn't the opponents—it's the 2GB RAM device facing lag spikes, white screen glitches, and auto shutdown.

LVL 07

The Merit Reward

Academic excellence in 10th yields a new tablet: 4GB RAM / 64GB ROM. Real achievement unlocking game hardware.

LVL 08

The Anime Awakening & Sharingan Technique

Naruto across Telugu $\rightarrow$ English $\rightarrow$ Japanese + Subs. Simultaneously parsing visual, subtitle, and audio channels. The legendary Exam Eve Naruto ritual.

LVL 09

Classroom Restart & The ID-Cardless Employee

Shift to ZPHS Government school. Entering marks, managing school databases, handling computer operations ahead of time.

LVL 10

Board Examination Triumph (92%)

Completing 10th standard with a stellar 92%, proving focus and discipline amid gaming and anime passions.

LVL 11

The Engineering Path (Diploma)

Polytechnic Diploma. Six semesters of labs, electronics, and logic. The timeless realization: Practicals stay forever.
