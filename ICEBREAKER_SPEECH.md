# 🎤 TOASTMASTERS ICE BREAKER SPEECH

## **"Every Legend Has a Beginning"**
**Speaker:** Yahoshuva
**Time:** 4–6 minutes
**Tone:** Serious opening → Humor in the middle → Powerful ending

---

> [!TIP]
> **Stage Directions** are in *[italics in brackets]*. Pauses are marked with ⏸️. Humor beats are marked with 😄.

---

## THE SCRIPT

*[Walk to the stage. Stand still. Look at the audience. Take a breath. Begin slowly.]*

---

**Every legend has a beginning.**

Some begin in extraordinary places — palaces, laboratories, secret military bases.

Mine began in a small town called **Sattenapalli**, in Andhra Pradesh, India.

⏸️

Why Sattenapalli? Because my native village — **Hassanabad** — didn't even have a hospital.

*[Slight smile]*

So technically, my very first journey in life... was a road trip. ⏸️ Before I could even open my eyes.

---

13th March, 2007. That was the day I entered the world.

And for the next few years, I was... ordinary.

I went to school. I sat in classrooms. I copied homework — *[pause]* — I mean... I **did** homework. 😄

*[Wait for laughter]*

I was Level 1. No special abilities. No extraordinary achievements. Just a kid trying to figure out how the world works.

---

And then...

⏸️

2020 happened.

*[Serious tone]*

The world shut down. Schools closed. Streets went empty. A pandemic locked 1.37 billion students inside their homes.

And there I was. Stuck in my bedroom. With one Android tablet. **2 GB of RAM. 32 GB of storage.**

*[Look at the audience]*

That tablet became my universe.

---

First, I discovered **gaming**.

I started small — Subway Surfers, Temple Run — you know, the classics. 😄

Then I found **LIMBO** — a dark, silent puzzle game with no instructions. You just... figure it out. Or die trying. Literally.

And then... I discovered **Free Fire**.

*[Lean in, like sharing a secret]*

Now let me tell you something about running a competitive battle royale on a 2 GB RAM tablet.

⏸️

The frame rate? ⏸️ **Single digits.**

Mid-fight? ⏸️ **White screen.**

Intense moments? ⏸️ The tablet would just... **switch itself off.** 😄

*[Gesture: phone shutting down in hand]*

I called that the **Hardware Boss Battle** — and I was losing. 😄

*[Wait for laughter]*

But I kept playing. Because I was learning something without realizing it — **problem-solving under pressure, strategy, and the ability to stay calm when everything around you is literally crashing.**

---

Then came the second portal — **Anime.**

My first anime was **Naruto**.

I started watching it in Telugu — my native language. When the dubbed episodes ended, I switched to English. When the English dub ran out...

*[Pause for effect]*

...I switched to **Japanese audio with English subtitles.**

⏸️

Do you know what it takes to follow rapid Japanese dialogue, read English subtitles at the bottom, AND watch the fight scene happening on screen — all at the same time?

*[Tap the side of your head]*

I called it the **Sharingan Technique.** 😄

If you know Naruto, you know exactly what I mean.

---

And here's the part that surprises people.

The night before my **10th standard board examination** — the biggest exam of my school life —

I finished my revision. ⏸️ Put my books away. ⏸️

And watched **one episode of Naruto** before going to sleep.

*[Smile]*

I scored **92 percent.**

*[Let it land]*

Gaming and anime didn't destroy my academics. They **coexisted** with discipline.

---

But life had more in store.

When I transferred to a **government school — ZPHS** — something unexpected happened.

The school needed someone to handle the computer lab. Enter data. Calculate marks. Manage student records.

They didn't hire anyone. They gave the job to **me** — a student.

*[Count on fingers]*

No job title. No employee ID. No salary.

*[Look up]*

Just a kid doing a professional's job. I called myself the **"ID-Cardless Employee."** 😄

And that experience? It was the most valuable salary I never received.

---

*[Shift to a more reflective tone]*

After school, I made a choice that most people questioned.

Instead of the traditional path — Intermediate, then a degree — I chose **Polytechnic Diploma in Engineering.**

Why?

Because I had figured something out by then.

*[Slow down. Deliver with weight.]*

**"Subjects can be cleared. But the practicals you have learnt... stay with you forever."**

⏸️

Textbooks gave me marks. But the labs — the circuits, the hardware, the code — they gave me **skills.**

---

*[Strongest voice now]*

Today, I'm building something.

It's called **YAHOSHUVA: ORIGIN ZERO.**

It's a game — a cinematic, open-world RPG — based on **my own life.**

Every level I just told you about? It's a chapter in the game. Every struggle is a boss battle. Every growth moment is an upgrade.

Because I believe that an ordinary life — lived with curiosity, courage, and the willingness to fail —

⏸️

...is an **extraordinary story** worth telling.

---

*[Final pause. Look across the entire room.]*

A wise man once said:

**"Ever tried. Ever failed. No matter. Try again. Fail again. Fail better."**

⏸️

That's not just a quote to me. That's my operating system.

⏸️

My name is **Yahoshuva**.

I started with no special powers. No advantages. No clear path.

But every legend has a beginning.

⏸️

And **this** — is mine.

*[Nod. Step back. Done.]*

## 🎯 DELIVERY TIPS

> [!IMPORTANT]
> ### Key Moments to Nail
> 1. **"My first journey was a road trip"** — deliver deadpan, let the audience catch it
> 2. **"I copied homework... I mean, I DID homework"** — classic self-correction humor, commit to it
> 3. **Hardware Boss Battle sequence** — build each line slower: "Frame rate? ... White screen? ... Switch itself off." Let each pause get longer
> 4. **"Sharingan Technique"** — tap your temple, this is your signature moment
> 5. **"92 percent"** — drop it like a mic drop. Let the number speak
> 6. **"ID-Cardless Employee"** — count on your fingers for "no title, no ID, no salary"
> 7. **"Fail better" quote** — slowest delivery of the entire speech. Each phrase is its own sentence
> 8. **"This is mine"** — look at the audience, not the floor. Own it

> [!TIP]
> ### Voice & Pacing
> - **First 30 seconds:** Slow, quiet, cinematic — pull them in
> - **Gaming & Anime sections:** Speed up, be animated, have fun 😄
> - **Last 45 seconds:** Slow back down. Drop your voice. Every. Word. Matters.
> - **Eye contact:** Pick 3 people in different parts of the room. Rotate between them
> - **Hands:** Use them for the hardware boss (phone shutting down gesture) and Sharingan (temple tap). Otherwise, keep them natural
